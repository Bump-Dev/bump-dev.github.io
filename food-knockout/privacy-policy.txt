Privacy Policy for Food Knockout

Effective Date: 19/8/2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Food Knockout handles user information.

Information We Collect

We do not personally collect, store, or share any personal data from players of Food Knockout.

However, the game displays advertisements provided by Appodeal and its mediated ad partners. These third-party services may automatically collect certain information, such as:

Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)

IP address

General location (non-precise)

Interactions with ads (e.g., impressions, clicks)

This information is used solely for delivering and personalizing ads, tracking performance, and ensuring compliance with legal requirements.

Children’s Privacy

Food Knockout is child-friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Appodeal has been configured to serve only child-appropriate ads.

Third-Party Services

The game uses Appodeal to mediate ads from multiple ad providers. For more details, please see Appodeal’s Privacy Policy:
👉 https://www.appodeal.com/privacy-policy

Your Choices

Depending on your device and region, you may have the ability to:

Opt out of personalized ads via device settings (e.g., “Opt out of Ads Personalization” on Android).

Manage consent choices through the in-game consent form (if shown).
